GSE310871 Dataset Audit

1. Total samples:137

2. Groups and sample sizes:
   Control:29
   Early CAD: 80
   Late CAD: 28

3. Biological sample: plasma
   
4. What exactly was sequenced? miRNA

5. What does the downloadable CSV contain? 287 rows of miRNA IDs (Column is patient samples, rows is individual miRNA)

6. Original study question: does circulating miRNAs can serve as effective diagnostic biomarkers for predicting early-onset CAD?

7. One possible confounding variable:smoking, obesity, diabetes mellitus, hypertension and dyslipidaemia

8. One limitation of this dataset: relatively small sample size, the early CAD group had a
significantly smaller number of female patients with CAD
